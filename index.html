Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

Index does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and it does not track you. Your library stays on your devices. To show you the news, Index connects directly to the websites you choose to follow.

Your library

The sources and categories you follow, the articles Index downloads, what you have read, your saved articles, your reading position and your settings are stored only inside the app on your device. They are removed when you delete the app.

Connections to the websites you follow

Index downloads feeds, article text, images and site icons directly from the publishers of the sources you follow or add. Like any app or browser that opens a website, these requests reveal your device's IP address and the address being requested to that website, which handles them under its own privacy policy. Index does not send these requests through any server of ours.

When you open an article's original page inside Index, the page is loaded in a private browsing session: cookies and website data from that session are not kept by Index after you close the app. If you choose Open in Safari, the page opens in Safari under Safari's settings.

Suggested sources

Suggestions come from a list built into the app. Your category names and choices are never sent anywhere to find them.

iCloud Drive

If you choose an iCloud Drive folder in Settings, Index writes your categories, sources, saved articles, read status and reading positions to that folder so your devices can stay in sync. This data is stored in your own iCloud account by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We have no access to it. If you do not choose a folder, nothing is written to iCloud.

No accounts, analytics or advertising

Index has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Contacting us

If you send feedback by email or through TestFlight, we receive only what you choose to include, and we use it only to reply and improve the app.

Children

Index does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to doudesukabrotha@gmail.com. If you are testing Index through TestFlight, you can also take a screenshot and send it as feedback.

Please include your device model, iOS version and Index version (shown in the App Store or TestFlight).

Tips

  • To follow a site, type its address in Add feed. A name without a domain, like “macstories”, is looked up as macstories.com. If a site ends differently, such as .co.uk or .it, type the full address.
  • Each category suggests sources from Index's own list. Open the category menu, choose Manage library, and tap + next to a suggestion.
  • In a list, swipe right to save an article and left to mark it as read. Unread only in the category menu hides what you have already read.
  • Swipe left on an article to open the publisher's page, and right to go back to the list.
  • Index removes unsaved articles after 3 days by default. Change this in Settings → Article retention. Saved articles are always kept.
  • To use Index on more than one device, choose the same iCloud Drive folder in Settings on each of them.
